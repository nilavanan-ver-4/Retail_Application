How tax works

  1. Set the tax label, registration number and default rate for new products.
  2. Assign the correct rate to existing products below. Use 0% for untaxed products.
  3. Create a sale. Product rates are applied after discounts and added to the selling price.
  4. Review the saved rate and amount on the invoice, print view, PDF or tax summary.

Saving settings does not change existing product rates. Product rate changes apply to future sales. Issued invoices retain their saved tax amounts and registration details.
